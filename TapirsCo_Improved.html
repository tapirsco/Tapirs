01 Custom Orders

Made to order. No returns, refunds or exchanges unless there is a defect.

02 Turnaround

3–7 working days. Bulk orders of 20+ may take longer.

03 Deposit & Payment

A 30% deposit secures production. Balance is due before delivery or pickup. BIBD / Baiduri + Cash, in BND.

04 Pricing

Prices are in BND. Extra-large, multi-colour or complex pieces may vary. Each order includes a box, thank-you card and round sticker.

05 Revisions

One free minor revision — spelling or colour swap — before printing. Major revisions are BND 5–15.

06 Pickup & Delivery

BSB self-pickup is FREE in Sengkurong / Bandar. Brunei-wide delivery is BND 3–8. Please check your order on pickup.

07 3D Printing Notes

Layer lines, light stringing and small colour variation are normal and are not defects.

08 Liability & Care

Do not expose PLA to sun or a car dashboard above 50°C. PETG is available on request. Keep NFC tags away from water.
